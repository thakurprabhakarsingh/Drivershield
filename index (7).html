01 / Problem

One driver. Multiple risks.

Fatigue, distraction, impaired driving and excessive speed can build up during a journey. Watching them separately leaves gaps.

Driver drowsiness

Fatigue reduces attention and reaction time.

Distraction

Losing visual attention creates dangerous situations in seconds.

Excessive speed

Speed needs continuous monitoring, not a review after an incident.

Delayed emergency response

After a serious event, finding the vehicle and understanding what happened is hard.

    05 / Core technology

    The BLACKBOX remembers what happened.

    When Blackbox Mode is on, the app takes a 10-second photo/video clip every 3 minutes and uploads it, encrypted, to a private cloud vault. If an accident happens, your saved emergency contacts can request the footage. Every clip is deleted automatically after 3 days.

    📸 Auto capture10 seconds every 3 minutes, even when the app is closed.
    🔐 Encrypted uploadEncrypted on the phone before it leaves, and stored encrypted.
    🆘 Accident-only accessFootage stays locked until an accident or SOS is detected.
    👤 Verified contactsOnly the emergency contacts you added, confirmed by a one-time code.
    🗓️ 3-day auto-deleteEvery clip has a 3-day timer, then it is erased from the cloud.
    📜 Access logYou can see who asked for what, and when.

    Privacy by design

    Private by defaultNo one, including the developer team, can browse your clips. Nothing is shared or sold.
    Encrypted end to endClips are encrypted before upload. Keys stay with you and are released only for an approved request.
    Least accessOnly footage around the accident is shared, through a link that expires in minutes.
    You stay in controlTurn it off any time, delete clips early, and review every access in the log.

    Design plan for the product. Real privacy depends on how the backend is built and audited. Recording a car cabin also involves other people, so passengers should be told, and the feature should follow local law (for example India's DPDP Act 2023) and keep clips only as long as needed.
